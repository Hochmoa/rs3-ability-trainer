Keybinds

Keys belong to a bar position and slot, exactly like in the game: whatever preset is shown there fires on that key. Click a slot, then press the key or combination. Modifiers show as c (Ctrl), s (Shift), a (Alt), e.g. c+3. Esc cancels, Backspace or the × clears. A key can only be bound once; binding it again moves it and frees the old slot. Browser-reserved combos (Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, F5, …) won't work reliably. Fill the presets on the Action bars page.
