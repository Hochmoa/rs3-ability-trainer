Boss presets

Gear and rotations from the PvME boss guides (PvM Encyclopedia Discord). "Add" creates a loadout with the preset's worn gear and backpack, imports the guide's rotations and puts the abilities they use on free action bars bound to the style – nothing of yours is replaced. Food, brews, familiars and other things the trainer does not simulate are left out.
